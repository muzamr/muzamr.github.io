INTRODUCTION

I am a Quality Assurance leader with more than ten years of experience across telecom, banking, consumer loyalty, and automotive services. Today I lead QA at Pep Boys, now part of Mavis Tire, where I own QA strategy, execution, and release readiness sign-off for four product streams that support store operations, technicians, and customers across roughly 800 stores nationwide.

Over the past three years, my team and I have supported more than 200 releases while our release cadence doubled. Along the way, we cut regression execution time by 80% with a new Playwright and RestAssured automation framework and reduced production defect leakage from every release to about one in four. When testing for our Verifone POS payment terminal release fell behind schedule, I stepped in to drive it to completion, helping the rollout reach 800 stores in five days. I also defined the initial test strategy for Carside, a new store application now running on about 1000+ tablets.

I believe lasting quality comes from people and process as much as from tools. I train and onboard offshore QA engineers, and I enjoy helping both offshore developers and QA engineers understand the business logic and end-to-end flows behind the applications we support. I work closely with engineering leadership, product, and project management to bring testing earlier into the lifecycle, set clear quality gates, and give decision-makers honest, data-driven insight before every release.

I am also exploring how AI can strengthen quality engineering responsibly. I designed and built an AI-assisted test design pipeline that turns Jira tickets into traceable requirements and test cases, with human approval gates, source citations, and automated checks against hallucinated output. I am motivated by the opportunity to help organizations build quality into every stage of delivery and to grow teams that take pride in their work.
